# Crop planes

Cropping lets you analyze a subset of the image and can reduce processing requirements.

!!! info "Before you start"
    **Prerequisites:**

    - Planes data loaded in Irbis.

    **UI location:**

    - Main app: **Planes** tab, **Pre-processing** section.

## Steps

1. In **Pre-processing**, click **Crop**.
2. In the new window, adjust the red outline around the area you want to keep.
3. Press **Crop**.
4. To cancel, press **Cancel**.

## Related

- After cropping, continue to [Preprocess planes](preprocess-data.md) or [Create a primary 3D model](../model/reconstruct-primary-model.md).
